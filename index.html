About

A creator at the intersection of technology, creativity and everyday life.

Welcome to Eric Kinetyc — a digital creator channel exploring the intersection of technology, creativity and everyday life. This is a constantly evolving space where technology meets personal expression, creativity meets experimentation, and everyday experiences become part of the story.

I'm Eric Kinetyc — technology enthusiast, digital content creator, educator, creative explorer and lifelong learner documenting the things I discover, create, test, experience and learn.

Technology is what I explore.
Creativity is how I express myself.
Life is what I document.

Eric Kinetyc brings multiple interests together in one place. From artificial intelligence and emerging technology to cosplay, fitness, swimming, digital culture, creative projects and everyday experiences, KINETYC reflects the different sides of modern digital life.
