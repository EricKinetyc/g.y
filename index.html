About

A creator at the intersection of technology, creativity and everyday life.

I'm a Guyanese technology enthusiast, creator and digital explorer. My work moves between two worlds: the technical and the expressive. One day I'm writing about AI and cybersecurity, the next I'm in full cosplay armor on a competition stage, and the day after I'm underwater in a pool at sunrise.

KINETYC is where all of it lives. Not a niche. Not a single-topic brand. A living record of a creator in motion — documenting the things I discover, create and experience.
